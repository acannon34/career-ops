3. **CRO-staff relationships** — true gap. AC has C-suite relationships in Chief Data/AI and Business Services orgs, not CROs. *Mitigation:* Aetna Business Director experience inside a Fortune 20 buyer — knows how revenue orgs operate.
4. **Pure sales-enablement tooling depth (Highspot, Mindtickle, Gong)** — soft gap. Learnable in <60 days.

## C) Level Strategy

JD level: Head of (top of enablement function, ~VP-equivalent). AC level: Managing Director (Accenture). **Match.**

**Sell senior:** "I've built and run enablement at two different scales — 5K analyst practice readiness and a 600-member community. Both started from zero. I know what the operating system looks like, and I've done it inside a 9K-practitioner organization that ships every quarter." Lead with "from the ground up" — that's the exact phrase in the JD.

**If downleveled:** Decline unless OpenAI offers Head-equivalent scope and $350K+ base. The JD is unambiguous about seniority — there is no downleveled version.
