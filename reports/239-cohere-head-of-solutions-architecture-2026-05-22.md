2. **Direct CRM/HubSpot/sales-pipeline ownership** — soft gap. AC has owned program-pipeline rigor at $75M PMO scale but not seller-pipeline rigor.
3. **NLP/RAG-specific technical depth** — partial fit. AC has built RAG infrastructure ("semantic memory and recall systems (PostgreSQL, BGE embeddings, hybrid RRF search)") but is not a deep NLP researcher.
4. **"Track record of exceeding sales targets"** — true gap. AC has driven $150M expansion as a delivery-credibility outcome, not as a quota-carrying SA leader. *Mitigation:* "$150M is the measurable downstream — that's the SA-leader-equivalent metric in my world."
5. **Toronto-HQ company cultural fit** — minor. SF location is listed, AC US-based, but Cohere's center of gravity is Canada.

## C) Level Strategy

JD level: Head of (top of SA function). AC level: Managing Director. **Match.**

**Sell senior:** Position as "I've architected the operating system for AI delivery at $10M flagship scale and shipped multi-agent platforms in production at Fortune-scale clients. I've co-sold $150M of expansion. I know what good SA leadership looks like because I've been the buyer (Aetna), the partner (Accenture MD), and the builder (5 production AI platforms)."

**If downleveled:** Decline. Head-of role is the right altitude. A Senior SA / Principal SA seat would be a meaningful step backward and Cohere's offer at that level would also fall below comp target.
