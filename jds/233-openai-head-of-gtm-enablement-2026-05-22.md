The GTM Enablement team is building and scaling OpenAI's global enablement engine, responsible for building OpenAI's commercial muscle at scale. As OpenAI's products, customer base, and partner ecosystem expand at unprecedented speed, this team ensures every customer-facing role is equipped and continuously up-leveled to deliver real business value with frontier AI.

## About the Role

Seeking a Head of Sales Enablement to build, scale, and lead OpenAI's revenue enablement strategy and operating playbooks. Senior, high-visibility leadership role charged with architecting the end-to-end enablement approach, from onboarding and sales methodology to product readiness, manager excellence, and scalable field execution.

This leader will own the end-to-end enablement playbooks, processes and systems — from onboarding and role readiness to advanced technical and industry certifications, product launches, and continuous field excellence.

## In this role, you'll:
- Build and scale a world-class revenue enablement organization from the ground up, spanning seller onboarding, sales enablement, sales methodology/sales excellence, and manager enablement
- Develop and own the global seller enablement strategy, including sales, partner/alliances enablement, and international field activation
- Cultivate deep, trusted relationships with CRO staff and senior Revenue leaders; represent enablement at key forums (QBRs, SKOs)
- Drive end-to-end field readiness for launches and strategic initiatives — message and positioning, competitive readiness, talk tracks, objection handling, deal coaching
- Partner with Product and Marketing to shape GTM readiness
- Develop compelling narratives, training experiences, executive-facing content
- Build scalable systems, processes, playbooks, measurement frameworks — role-based certifications, onboarding journeys, KPI instrumentation
- Recruit, develop, and lead a high-performing team of enablement leaders

## We're seeking someone with:
- 15+ years of senior sales enablement / revenue enablement leadership experience at scaled, high-growth tech companies
- Track record of partnering directly with C-suite and executive stakeholders to drive measurable improvements in productivity, pipeline performance, forecasting
- Deeply connected across enablement ecosystem, methodology, coaching, learning design, sales ops/revops
- Strong executive presence, exceptional communication
- Previously built or led enablement at a high-growth company
- Ability to translate ambiguous, first-of-its-kind AI capabilities into clear, repeatable value propositions and selling motions
- High horsepower, strategic clarity, strong judgment
- Care about enabling selling motions that advance safe, human-centered AI deployment
