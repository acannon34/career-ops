## About the role

As a GTM Strategy & Operations Lead on the Special Projects team, you will operate as a strategic force multiplier across Anthropic's entire sales organization. Rather than embedding within a single segment, you'll tackle the highest-priority, cross-cutting initiatives that span our go-to-market motion—from Enterprise and Strategic Accounts to Startups, Commercial, Public Sector, and Partnerships. You'll lead analytical deep-dives, build frameworks that scale across segments, and drive projects from scoping through execution that directly shape how we grow revenue and serve customers.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives on variety and ambiguity—moving between a segmentation redesign one week, an AE performance analysis the next, and a cross-functional process overhaul after that. You'll report to the GTM Strategy & Operations lead and partner closely with segment Business Partners, Revenue Operations, and GTM leadership.

## Responsibilities

**Strategic Analysis & Cross-Segment Projects**
- Lead analytical deep-dives on segmentation strategy, coverage models, resource allocation, and capacity planning across all GTM segments
- Build and maintain frameworks (account prioritization models, territory scoring, pipeline health diagnostics) designed to scale across segments and geographies
- Own end-to-end special projects from scoping and stakeholder alignment through execution and impact measurement
- Develop executive-ready deliverables for QBRs, board materials, and leadership offsites

**Operational Infrastructure & Scalability**
- Design processes and playbooks that help the GTM team scale through hypergrowth, including leveraging Claude for automation
- Partner with Revenue Operations to improve CRM data quality, reporting accuracy, and systems of record
- Build and maintain cross-segment dashboards and KPIs

**Data-Driven Decision Support**
- Collaborate with data science on predictive models (account scoring, propensity-to-buy)
- Analyze pipeline trends, conversion rates, top-of-funnel metrics
- Conduct AE and team performance analyses
- Support Finance on market sizing and resource investment modeling

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**
- Connective thread between segment Business Partners
- Partner with Marketing, Partnerships, Applied AI teams
- Work with Product and Engineering on customer feedback patterns

## Minimum Qualifications
- Experience in SaaS/enterprise software with focus on analytics, revenue/business operations, strategy consulting, or finance
- Track record of owning cross-functional projects in fast-paced, ambiguous environments
- Strong analytical skills with CRM, analytics platforms, BI tools (Salesforce, Looker, BigQuery)
- Project management and stakeholder influence skills
- Bachelor's degree required

## Preferred Qualifications
- Deep proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets; SQL or Python preferred
- Experience with consumption-based pricing models and usage analytics
- Building scalable frameworks/processes/playbooks across teams
