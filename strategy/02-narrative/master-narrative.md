# 02 — Narrative

The spine. Everything else hangs off this. One narrative, written to flex two ways:

- To **operating partners / search firms**: "owns a P&L and named outcomes"
- To **future clients (independent)**: the same proof points, repackaged as a point of view

The shift that matters: **from advisor to owner.** Both destinations buy someone who has carried a number and lived with the consequences — not someone who advised on it. Rewrite every accomplishment in that language.

> **Don't start from a blank page.** Your verified proof points already live in
> [`career-ops/modes/_profile.md`](../../modes/_profile.md) — Ground Truth facts (P&Ls,
> $150M expansion, $30M savings, scale numbers), the archetype→proof-point map, the exit
> narrative, and comp targets. That file is aimed at AI-lab roles. The job here is to
> **re-frame the same proof for PE operating-partner / portfolio-exec buyers** — owner of a
> number, not advisor on one. Pull from `_profile.md`; this file is the PE-framed layer on top.
