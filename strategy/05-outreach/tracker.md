# 05 — Outreach

Phase 3 (month 4–7) is **conversations, not applications.** At your level, looking eager is a liability; being selective is the signal of value. The frame is always exploratory and senior — "open to the right thing," not "looking."

## Message templates

*(Drafts — personalize heavily. Keep senior and low-pressure.)*

### To a peer who went PE-side
```
[Reconnect, genuine interest in their move, ask to hear how the operating-partner world actually works.
Not "are you hiring" — "I'd value your read on the transition."]
```

### To a search firm contact
```
[Introduce, position with the narrative, signal you're exploratory and selective.
"Building a relationship before there's a mandate" — not "looking now."]
```

### To a potential anchor client (independent)
```
[Lead with the POV/value, not the ask. Test demand: would this be useful to you?
Stay inside your non-solicit.]
```
