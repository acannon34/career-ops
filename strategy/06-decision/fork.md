# 06 — Decision: The Fork

The two tracks **share the first ~4 months** (covenant review, narrative, build-in-public) and then **diverge hard:**

- **PE** = join a structure. A search process.
- **Independent** = leave all structure. A soft launch.

You don't have to pick yet. Running both in parallel through month 4 gives you leverage — a credible independent option is the best negotiating position walking into PE conversations, and vice versa. **Let the market help you choose** rather than deciding in the abstract: the fork resolves when one track produces real signal (a portfolio mandate that fits, or anchor clients saying yes).

But decide *now* what would make each a "yes," so you're not deciding under the pressure of a live offer.

## The honest tension

These pull in opposite directions: one is *joining* a structure, one is *leaving all* structures. The shared foundation work masks that for a while, but around month 4–5 you'll feel it. Worth sitting with: which version of the next 5 years do you actually want — owning a seat in someone's value-creation machine, or owning the whole thing yourself?

## Fork criteria (the trigger)

Move from "both in parallel" to "commit to one" when:
- A PE mandate clears your YES bar, **OR**
- 2–3 anchor clients commit and runway is set, **OR**
- Month 5 arrives and you choose deliberately rather than drift.

- [ ] Notice given professionally; relationships protected
- [ ] No burned bridges — consulting and PE worlds are small, reputation arrives first
- [ ] Transition your Accenture responsibilities cleanly
