# 03 — Track: PE / Operating Roles

**Frame: join a structure.** Operating partner, portfolio-company exec, or value-creation leadership inside a fund.

The hidden requirement: PE firms buy people who have *owned outcomes*, not advised on them. Your practice P&L is your proof. The other thing they reward — and you have an edge here — is evidence you can *create demand*, which is exactly what the build-in-public work (see `04-track-independent/`) signals.

The entry point is **mostly off-market.** These roles fill fast and quietly through operating-partner networks and the search firms funds actually use. The goal for months 2–4 is to be a known quantity in those pipelines *before* a specific mandate opens.

## Diligence checklist (when something goes live)

Run real diligence on the *fund and role*, not just the pitch:
- [ ] Fund: vintage, dry powder, hold period, track record
- [ ] Role: actual scope and authority vs. the title
- [ ] Equity: structure, vesting, what it's really worth at exit (not headline)
- [ ] The people: who you'd report to, portfolio CEO dynamics
- [ ] Realistic value-creation thesis — can you actually move the number here?
