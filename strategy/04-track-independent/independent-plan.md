# 04 — Track: Build / Independent

**Frame: leave all structure.** Solo advisory, a small firm you build, or a productized IP play.

The constraint here isn't discoverability — it's **demand generation and the economics of going solo.** Three things PE doesn't make you think about:

1. **Covenant exposure is worse, not better.** Going independent and calling your old clients is exactly what the client non-solicit was written to stop. Your book *is* your relationships, so `01-foundation/` gates this even harder. Know which relationships you can legally pursue, and when.
2. **The model choice is upstream of everything.** Pick before you build (see below) — the runway, cash needs, and risk are completely different.
3. **Building in public is the engine, not a signal.** The POV you publish is *literally how clients find you* once Accenture's brand is gone. (It happens to also help the PE track — same activity, but here it's load-bearing.)

## A. Model choice (decide this first)

| Model | What it is | Margin | Capped by | Runway/risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo advisory** | You, billing your time | High | Your hours | Lowest risk, capped upside |
| **Small firm** | You build & staff for leverage | Medium | Your ability to sell + hire | Higher risk, now running a business |
| **Productized / IP** | Frameworks & POV sold repeatedly | Highest (scales) | Demand for the product | Hardest to start, best ceiling |

The core question: are you selling **your time** or building **something that runs without you**?
