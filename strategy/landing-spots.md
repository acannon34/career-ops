## 2. Enterprise transformation (this addition)

**Wide net — both flavors:**

- **AI-led transformation:** Chief AI Officer, Head of AI Transformation, VP AI Enablement, Head of Applied AI
- **Broad / digital transformation:** Chief Transformation Officer, Head of Enterprise Transformation, SVP/VP Strategy & Operations, VP Digital Transformation

**Target sectors** (from `_profile.md`, in order): Consumer/Retail, Healthcare (payer/provider/gov), Public Sector, enterprise tech, management consulting. **No Financial Services.**

**Positioning:** Use the "Transformation / Strategy roles" playbook and "AI Transformation" archetype already in [`../modes/_profile.md`](../modes/_profile.md). Lead with operating-model design authority, the 1,300-person org redesign, 8 global markets, and C-suite advisory. AI is the enabler, not the product. Keep the full process-architecture detail (29 steps, 5 phases, 4 archetypes, 6 governance dimensions).

**Sourcing — two channels, with a real constraint:**

1. **Scanner (`../portals.yml`):** title filter already whitelists `transformation`, `strategy`, `enterprise`, `operating model`, and now `chief`. The gap is *companies* — the scanner only scrapes Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever boards. Most large enterprises run Workday, which it can't read. So automated coverage skews to tech-forward enterprises, health-tech, gov-tech, and consultancies on supported ATSes. A verified company list is being added to `tracked_companies`.
2. **Network / executive search:** because of the Workday gap, many of the best enterprise transformation roles won't show up in the scanner at all. These are sourced the same way as the PE track — warm intros and the retained search firms (see `05-outreach/`). Treat the scanner as partial coverage, not the whole funnel.

> Reality check: enterprise transformation leadership at Workday-shop Fortune 500s is largely an off-scanner, network-driven search. The scanner catches the ATS-friendly subset; the network catches the rest.
